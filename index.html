About this browser version: it stores text in a small, reversible RGB pixel residual (at most one color level per changed channel) and exports a lossless PNG. This is a lightweight client-side method, not the trained neural StegaStamp model. Resizing, editing, or JPEG-compressing the encoded image can damage the message. Keep the output as PNG.
No uploads, accounts, or server-side processing required.
